Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

KageBound is a single-player game by Fatih Beser. The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services. The game does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No in-app purchases.
  • The game makes no network connections; it plays fully offline.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, export or delete on our side. The game is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone. If this policy changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.

Support

Questions or bug reports: please open an issue at github.com/fbeser/kagebound-privacy/issues.
